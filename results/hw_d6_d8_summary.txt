Notes
- Stopband results (D6, D7 500 Hz) bottom out near -125 to -130 dBFS: the residue in that bin after the
  filter, i.e. the measurement floor of a 16 384-sample capture, not the filter. They show >= 85 dB rejection.
- Pass-band and transition-band results agree with the design within the acoustic repeatability
  (repeated bank-0 runs moved by up to 5 dB when the setup was touched, 0.6 dB when it was not).
- The laptop speaker produces little at 200 Hz (-55 dBFS) and its 2nd/3rd harmonics (400, 600 Hz) fall in the
  band-pass, so bank 3 at 200 Hz shows strong 400/600 Hz lines: expected, those are in the pass band.
- Bank 2 at 500 Hz: the remaining 2.5-3.4 kHz noise hump is room noise in the pass band (2 kHz and up).
