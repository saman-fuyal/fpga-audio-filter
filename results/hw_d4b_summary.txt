Conclusions
1. THD falls by 16 dB as the phone volume is lowered by 12 dB: the distortion is level dependent, i.e. a
   nonlinearity in the acoustic source. The FPGA path is linear (bit-exact on the board, C3/C4).
2. At the same microphone level (about -32 dBFS) the laptop speaker gives THD -37.6 dB vs -14.7 dB for the
   phone: 23 dB less with the same microphone and FPGA, so the phone speaker caused the -15 dBc 3rd harmonic.
3. Mic + FPGA chain THD at -32 dBFS is therefore at most -37.6 dB (1.3 %); an upper bound, because the
   laptop speaker still contributes. Paper values (make_macros.py): SNR 42.2 dB, THD -37.6 dB, SINAD 36.3 dB.
4. LED meter: -4.7 dB -> one LED fewer, -11.8 dB -> two fewer, i.e. about 6 dB per LED, as in D2.
5. SNR (33-49 dB) is limited by room noise and mains hum in the 20 Hz - 20 kHz band, not by the chain.
6. D5 (SW6 up vs down, phone 1 kHz): same within 1 dB (THD -13.9 / -15.1 dB, SNR 34.7 / 35.5 dB) - PASS.
